An Integrated AI & IoT System for Coconut Plantation Monitoring

for Sustainable Agriculture in Sri Lanka

Research Overview

Coconut (Cocos nucifera) is an essential economic and dietary crop in Sri Lanka, yet commercial estates and smallholders encounter severe yield loss stemming from foliar diseases, undetected nutrient deficiencies, extreme climatic shifts, and an absence of tailored agronomical advisory support. Traditional estate management relies heavily on manual, infrequent visual inspections and historical rule-of-thumb practices, leading to delayed interventions and excessive crop loss.

To address these multifaceted challenges, SaruPol introduces an end-to-end intelligent precision agriculture ecosystem that unifies macroscopic aerial surveillance, handheld microscopic pathology diagnostics, AI-guided nutrient assessment, IoT-enabled microclimatic telemetry, and trilingual expert advisory.

By integrating edge-optimized deep learning models (MobileNetV2 INT8 and YOLOv8) with solar-powered dual-node ESP32 field telemetry and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) referencing Coconut Research Institute (CRI) standards, SaruPol empowers growers with real-time proactive warnings, precision fertilizer dosage recommendations, and bio-agronomic yield prognostics.

Overall, the system provides a scalable, farmer-accessible solution engineered for resource-constrained plantation environments, modernizing sustainable coconut management and productivity across Sri Lanka.

Research Scope

This section presents the background study, identified research gaps, and the core problem addressed by the SaruPol system for coconut plantation management.

Literature Survey

Traditional coconut plantation inspection primarily relies on manual visual examination by tree climbers and plantation officers, which is slow, subjective, and prone to late detection. In tall perennial trees like coconut palms, foliar diseases (such as Bud Rot, Leaf Rot, Stem Bleeding, and Gray Leaf Spot) frequently go unnoticed until irreversible crown collapse occurs.

Recent advancements in computer vision and deep learning have introduced automated agricultural disease detection and aerial surveillance. UAV-based RGB and multispectral monitoring have emerged as promising approaches for macroscopic health mapping. However, high-altitude aerial imagery struggles to detect fine, microscopic foliar lesions, while standalone handheld diagnostic applications lack aerial context, geospatial disease dispatching, and out-of-distribution noise filtering.

Furthermore, existing precision agriculture platforms treat soil nutrient management, microclimatic telemetry, and diagnostic advisory as disconnected silos. Soil telemetry systems rarely feed directly into agronomic yield prognostics, and standard LLM farmer chatbots frequently hallucinate remedies because they are not grounded in validated, circular-indexed standards from institutions like the Coconut Research Institute of Sri Lanka (CRI).

Research Gap

Despite recent advancements in digital agriculture, significant research gaps persist in perennial coconut plantation management in Sri Lanka:

1. Macroscopic-Microscopic Disconnect: Aerial drone surveys efficiently highlight estate-wide canopy stress hotspots but cannot perform leaf-level pathogen confirmation. Conversely, handheld smartphone apps diagnose isolated leaves but lack spatial dispatch coordination to locate infected palms across vast acreage.

2. Out-of-Distribution Foliar Fragility: Standard mobile disease classifiers fail when presented with non-foliar field noise (fingers, soil, ground weeds) because they lack temperature-scaled uncertainty rejection.

3. Sensor Data Isolation & Absence of Bio-Agronomic Yield Prognostics: Soil sensor deployments record raw abiotic time-series without fusing them with biological palm age, variety, and historical yield metrics into calibrated forward prognostics.

4. Unlocalized Agronomic Advice: Conventional extension services and generic AI models lack real-time integration with Coconut Research Institute (CRI) Differential Fertilizer Recommendation (DFR) circulars in local languages (Sinhala and Tamil).

Research Problem

Coconut plantations in Sri Lanka face substantial productivity losses due to undetected disease outbreaks (Bud Rot, Leaf Rot, Stem Bleeding), severe nutrient imbalances, extreme weather variability, and a lack of timely, localized agronomical guidance. Smallholders and estate managers predominantly rely on manual visual checks and traditional rule-of-thumb knowledge, resulting in delayed interventions, wasted fertilizers, and declining nut yields.

Furthermore, existing precision farming technologies fail to provide a unified, farmer-accessible workflow. Aerial drone surveys remain detached from ground inspections; mobile disease diagnostics lack reliability under field noise; environmental sensor logs are rarely translated into biological yield projections; and expert advice from national bodies like the Coconut Research Institute (CRI) remains trapped in physical manuals that are inaccessible during field crises.

Therefore, there is an urgent need for an integrated, multimodal AI & IoT ecosystem that connects macroscopic aerial surveillance, handheld microscopic diagnostics, automated nutrient estimation, microclimatic telemetry, and expert RAG advisory into a single, cohesive plantation management system.

Research Objectives & Four Pillars

A multimodal AI-IoT ecosystem integrating edge monitoring, vision-based health mapping, and RAG-driven expert advisory for the S A R U P O L ecosystem.

Component 01 • IT22546920 (Lakshan H.G.J.S.)

Dual-Scale Pathology Detection & Biosecurity Bridge

Implements a multi-scale computer vision framework bridging macroscopic aerial drone surveillance with microscopic mobile leaf diagnostics.

System A (Aerial): Orthomosaic processing with Excess-Green (ExG) masking, Euclidean Distance Transform (EDT) crown peak extraction (97.6% F1-score), and Moving-Window Spatial Z-score anomaly hotspot mapping.

System B (Microscopic):Lightweight on-device MobileNetV2 INT8 model (91.42% accuracy, <50ms inference) classifying 6 major foliar pathologies, paired with Temperature-Scaled Shannon Entropy out-of-distribution rejection and automated CRI treatment lookup.

Component 02 • IT22220424 (Kaushalya P.L.P.D.)

Nutrient Deficiency Assessment & DFR Recommendation

Provides automated, vision-driven foliar nutrient diagnostics and national-standard fertilizer computation to eliminate excessive agrochemical waste.

YOLOv8 Foliar Classification: Evaluates palm leaf coloration and necrosis characteristics to identify primary nutrient deficiencies (Nitrogen, Potassium, Magnesium) with a 94.6% Top-1 classification accuracy.

CRI DFR Recommendation Engine: Combines image assessment and laboratory soil-leaf inputs with palm growth stage and agro-ecological zone data to calculate Differential Fertilizer Recommendation (DFR) mixtures and dosages.

Component 03 • IT22293930 (Jayasinghe Y.L.)

Hybrid AI & IoT Coconut Yield Prediction System

Transforms field telemetry into long-horizon harvest forecasts using dedicated hardware edge nodes and multi-tier ML regressors.

Dual-Node ESP32 Telemetry: Features Node 1 for micro-climate sensing (solar radiation, ambient temp, rainfall) and Node 2 for soil root-zone parameters (NPK, moisture, pH) with on-device OLED diagnostics.

Bio-Agronomic Scaling Engine: Fuses abiotic time-series with biological modifiers (palm variety, age, nut-drop counts) via a Tuned Random Forest Regressor, achieving 90.19% R² predictive accuracy (MAE = 0.7949 nuts/palm).

Component 04 • IT22070630 (Perera K.D.N.)

Trilingual RAG Precision Agricultural Advisory

Provides farmer-accessible, circular-grounded advisory in English, Sinhala, and Tamil via agentic retrieval-augmented generation.

CRI Knowledge Base: 1,507 FAISS-indexed vector chunks curated directly from official Coconut Research Institute advisory circulars and agronomic manuals.

Multi-LLM Consensus Pipeline: Integrates domain-aware translation cascade, pre-warming speech pipelines (Whisper + edge TTS), and multi-LLM consensus (GPT-4o mini, LLaMA 3.1, Gemini 2.5) with an 80% early-exit rate to minimize cloud costs while eliminating hallucinations (BERTScore = 0.9667).

Our Approach
Research Methodology

This section presents the overall solution architecture of the proposed SaruPol ecosystem, engineered as an integrated edge-to-cloud precision plantation platform. The architecture unifies four core pillars: dual-scale pathology detection, AI-guided nutrient assessment with national CRI Differential Fertilizer Recommendations (DFR), IoT microclimatic telemetry with bio-agronomic yield prognostics, and a trilingual retrieval-augmented advisory engine.

Figure 2: Overall System Architecture Diagram

Multi-source data ingestion captures environmental time-series via dual-node ESP32 sensors, drone orthomosaics, mobile leaf diagnostic images, and farmer speech queries. The cognitive engine synthesizes these inputs through lightweight quantized models (MobileNetV2 INT8 and YOLOv8), machine learning regressors (Random Forest), and a multi-LLM consensus pipeline referencing indexed Coconut Research Institute (CRI) standards.

Actionable outputs deliver spatiotemporal disease hotspot maps, custom fertilizer dosages, predictive harvest schedules, and trilingual voice advisories directly to smallholder growers and commercial estate superintendents.

Component 01 • IT22546920 (Lakshan H.G.J.S.)
Dual-Scale Coconut Pathology Detection & Edge Diagnostics

This component bridges macroscopic aerial canopy surveillance with offline-first microscopic edge mobile diagnostics, solving the scale disconnect in coconut plantation biosecurity without requiring expensive cloud infrastructure or persistent field connectivity.

Novelty 1: Deterministic UAV Crown Morphology (System A)

Canonical Excess-Green (ExG) chromatic filtering coupled with Euclidean Distance Transform (EDT) peak extraction detects 938 palm crowns in 100.9 ms without manual training labels, followed by local spatial Z-score filtering to isolate 21 early canopy stress hotspots.

Novelty 2: Full-Integer INT8 Quantized Edge Mobile Diagnostics (System B)

Asymmetric Full-Integer INT8 Post-Training Quantized MobileNetV2 compressed to 2.71 MB (75.0% reduction) achieves 91.42% accuracy across 6 pathological classes (WCLWD, Bud Rot, Stem Bleeding, Leaf Rot, Healthy, Flaccidity) with 11.76 ms CPU latency.

Novelty 3: Calibrated OOD Rejection & Offline-First Sync Bridge

Temperature-Scaled Shannon Entropy gating filters non-foliar visual noise as "Ambiguous Foliage", while an offline SQLite queue caches hotspot inspections and synchronizes CRI treatment protocols when connectivity is restored.

Figure 3: Dual-scale pathology detection architecture uniting macroscopic UAV surveys and edge mobile diagnostics.

The multiscale pathology framework directly addresses the scale gap in plantation biosecurity: macroscopic UAV flights pinpoint canopy stress clusters without label-hungry detectors, automatically dispatching offline field scouts running calibrated INT8 neural diagnostics on entry-level Android devices.

Component 02 • IT22220424 (Kaushalya P.L.P.D.)
Soil Nutrient Assessment & DFR Fertilizer Recommendation

This component provides automated deficiency symptom recognition (Nitrogen, Potassium, Magnesium, Boron) coupled with a soil fertility knowledge engine to formulate precise Coconut Research Institute (CRI) Differential Fertilizer Recommendations (DFR).

Figure 4: Nutrient assessment and CRI DFR prescription engine architecture.

The nutrient assessment framework bridges visual foliar symptom recognition with quantitative soil chemistry: edge-detected deficiency markers trigger automated CRI Differential Fertilizer calculations, generating customized, cost-optimized nutrient replenishment schedules aligned with monsoon rainfall patterns to prevent nutrient runoff and maximize palm yield.

Novelty 1: Foliar Nutrient Deficiency Recognition (YOLOv8)

Custom-trained YOLOv8 computer vision model achieving 94.6% Top-1 accuracy in identifying chlorosis and necroses associated with Nitrogen (N), Potassium (K), Magnesium (Mg), and Micronutrient (Boron) deficiencies under variable outdoor lighting conditions.

Novelty 2: CRI Differential Fertilizer Recommendation (DFR) Algorithm

Automated agronomic calculation integrating soil pH, soil texture class, organic carbon, and palm age tiers (seedling, young, bearing adult) to compute palm-specific urea, muriate of potash (MOP), rock phosphate (ERP), and dolomite quantities.

Novelty 3: Geo-Zoned Fertilizer Scheduling & Cost Optimization

Generates split-application schedules synchronized with monsoonal rain windows (Yala and Maha seasons) to minimize nutrient leaching while calculating cost projections against prevailing Sri Lankan agricultural retail prices.

Component 03 • IT22293930 (Jayasinghe Y.L.)
Hybrid AI & IoT-Driven Coconut Yield Prediction & Weather Analytics

This component models the 45-day coconut harvest developmental cycle by synthesizing real-time microclimatic telemetry, satellite meteorology, soil physics, and tree biometrics into a dual-model predictive yield architecture.

Novelty 1: Dual-Node Edge IoT Sensing Architecture

Solar-powered ESP32 microcontroller network capturing microclimatic canopy ambient temperature, relative humidity (DHT22), and root-zone capacitive volumetric soil moisture at 15-minute intervals with edge deduplication.

Novelty 2: Dual-Model Machine Learning Prognostic Pipeline

Couples an optimized Random Forest Regressor (100 estimators, max depth 15) achieving 90.19% R² with an LSTM sequential neural network (64 → 32 units with dropout regularization) modeling temporal stress accumulation across the 45-day bunch maturation window.

Novelty 3: Explainable Feature Importance & Yield Risk Classification

Computes SHAP-based feature attributions highlighting soil moisture thresholds and heat stress indices as dominant yield drivers, mapping predictions into commercial harvest classes (High, Moderate, Deficit) for supply chain planning.

Figure 5: Hybrid AI and IoT telemetry pipeline for 45-day coconut harvest yield forecasting.

The yield prediction framework transforms volatile agricultural production into predictable harvest metrics by synchronizing physical field telemetry with bio-climatic historical yield patterns, enabling forward-looking logistics for desiccated coconut and oil processing mills.

Component 04 • IT22070630 (Perera K.D.N.)
Trilingual RAG Precision Agricultural Advisory Engine

This component provides an authoritative conversational intelligence platform grounded in 1,507 indexed Coconut Research Institute (CRI) advisory guidelines, delivering hallucination-free guidance across Sinhala, Tamil, and English with voice synthesis.

Figure 6: Trilingual RAG advisory engine with multi-LLM consensus and CRI knowledge indexing.

The advisory framework overcomes agrarian literacy hurdles and language barriers by providing real-time voice and text consultation directly in local vernaculars, ensuring that official research-backed crop management protocols reach smallholder farming communities without distortion.

Novelty 1: Authoritative CRI Vector Indexing (FAISS)

1,507 curated agronomic passages sourced directly from official Coconut Research Institute (CRI) advisory circulars, soil management manuals, and pathology bulletins indexed into dense vector embeddings for high-precision semantic retrieval.

Novelty 2: Multi-LLM Consensus & Citation Engine

Cross-validates responses using a dual-LLM consensus pipeline that checks claims against retrieved CRI source chunks before delivering answers, eliminating generative hallucinations and providing exact circular citations.

Novelty 3: Trilingual Voice & Text Modality (Sinhala / Tamil / English)

Seamless multilingual natural language processing supporting native Sinhala (සිංහල), Tamil (தமிழ்), and English audio queries with automated speech-to-text and voice playback for accessible smallholder farmer engagement.
